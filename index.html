      Hard rules

      • SPF every morning, no exceptions — especially on a retinoid.
      • Never combine Differin with Topicals Faded, TreeActiv, or Sulfur on the same night.
      • Differin: buffered over moisturizer, pea-sized, whole face.
      • Spot treatments (TreeActiv, Sulfur) go on individual lesions only, never the whole face.
      • Apply serums to slightly damp skin — not soaking wet, not bone dry.
      • No picking — especially blood-filled cysts (scarring/PIE risk).
      • Water temperature: lukewarm only.
